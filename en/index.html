01 — About

We do three things.
Only three.

A non-profit corporation licensed in 2017 by the Korean Ministry of Trade, Industry and Energy. It grew out of a circle formed in 2002 by merchandisers from department stores and home shopping networks who shared their shelves and their buyers. Today the association does three things: bring the customer in, send the product out, and hold the shelf itself. Everything else has been set aside.

04 — Wholesale Centers

Wholesale centers,
run by the association itself

The association does not merely introduce a channel; it runs the shelf. There are no price tags on the floor, so members can open new trade without disturbing the prices their existing channels depend on. Wholesale, buyer matching and MD training all happen in one space.

A wholesale center — open to invited buyers and groups only

MD training inside the center — taught by merchandisers from major retailers

Wholesale

Members display; the association invites buyers and handles talks, interpreting and settlement. Quantity, lead time and customs are settled on the spot.

Buyer Matching

Trade briefings with regional business agencies, and overseas buyers brought onto the floor.

MD Training

Product planning, cost and margin, entry proposals, channel operations, live commerce, customs and certification.

Placement

Graduates go straight to member companies and to the center floor. Where a company needs a merchandiser, we assign one we trained.
